About Northfield

Built by people who hated broken cold chains.

Northfield started with three reefer trailers and a promise: if it's supposed to be frozen when it arrives, it will be frozen when it arrives. Three decades later, that promise still runs every lane on our board.

Our History

timeline
1994

Three Trailers, One Route

Northfield launches with a single frozen-freight lane between Chicago and Minneapolis, run out of a leased dock.

2003

First Regional Hub

Opening of the Chicago cross-dock facility allows Northfield to consolidate frozen and chilled freight under one roof.

Northfield's cold storage network earns validation for temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical distribution.

The lane network expands to cover all major U.S. metro corridors, with continuous digital temperature logging on every load.
